# Ante and uncalled return expectation

Hand 1009 is accepted. Each player pays a 1-cent ante. Ada pays a 1-cent small blind and calls 1 cent; Bo pays a 2-cent big blind and checks. Ada's 2-cent flop bet is returned after Bo folds. Each player's net contribution is 3 cents, making one 6-cent gross pot eligible only to Ada. Ada receives 6 cents and rake is zero. The ante does not count toward Ada's preflop street contribution.
