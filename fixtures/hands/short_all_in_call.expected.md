# Short all-in call expectation

Hand 1010 is accepted. Ada can call only 3 cents after her 1-cent small blind, below the 10-cent bet. Her total contribution is 4 cents; Bo and Cy each contribute 10 cents. The 12-cent main pot is eligible to all three seats and goes to Ada. The 12-cent side pot is eligible only to Bo and Cy and goes to Bo. There is no uncalled return or rake. Contributions of 24 cents exactly equal awards of 24 cents.
