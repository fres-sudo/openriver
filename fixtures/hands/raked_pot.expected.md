# Raked pot expectation

Hand 1006 is accepted. Ada and Bo contribute 5 USD cents each. The
single gross pot is 10 cents; Ada receives 9 cents and 1 cent is rake.
The raise-to amount is Ada's total preflop contribution, including her
small blind. The stated 3-cent raise is the increase from the 2-cent
outstanding bet to 5 cents.
